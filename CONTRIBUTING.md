# Contributing to Denarii DFi

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the **Denarii DFi Ecosystem**.

We welcome contributions from open-source developers, researchers, AI agent builders, and DLT engineers.

---

## Areas of Active Development

- **Agent Harnesses**: Minimal, deterministic cognitive harness implementations.
- **Cross-Chain Connectors**: Enhancements for XRPL, Algorand, Hedera, and Flare RPC clients.
- **Policy Firewalls**: Scoped spending limits, replay-resistant outbox patterns, and formal verification tests.
- **PQC Security**: Benchmarks and compatibility wrappers for NIST post-quantum cryptographic standards.

---

## How to Get Involved

1. Explore the live orchestrator at [denarii-orchestrator.netlify.app](https://denarii-orchestrator.netlify.app).
2. Review our technical documentation in the `docs/` folder.
3. Check open developer challenges on [One Million Developers](https://onemilliondevs.netlify.app).
4. Join the conversation on our [Discord Community](https://discord.gg/zCw4sCCDHD).

---

## Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors. Please treat fellow community members with respect and professionalism.
